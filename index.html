Tipoff

Permit intelligence for home-service contractors.

Akros Systems builds Tipoff, a weekly report of new residential building permits for contractors who want to reach homeowners before the job is awarded.

What we do

Tipoff collects public residential building-permit records from city and county open-data portals, identifies the type of work, and sends each customer the permits that match their trade and service area.

  • Weekly email reports with the newest matching permits
  • Permit details, job type and property information
  • Trade and ZIP-code filtering
  • Optional postcard packs for Territory customers

Who it is for

Home-service contractors including solar, HVAC, roofing, electrical, pool, landscaping, fencing, insulation and remodeling companies.
