Method and sources

How these pages are built

Every number on these pages comes from a city or county open-data feed, read again each week. Nothing is estimated or modeled.

Sources

  • Austin, TX: City of Austin open data: Issued Construction Permits. City of Austin permits, including its extraterritorial jurisdiction, classed as residential.
  • Collin County, TX: Collin Central Appraisal District: Building Permits (data.texas.gov). Residential permits that Frisco, Allen, McKinney, Wylie, Prosper, Celina and the other Collin County cities report to the appraisal district. Cities report permits to the appraisal district in monthly batches, so the latest weeks fill in late and the counts here run low until they do. For that reason we don't compare them with the weeks before.
  • Dallas, TX: City of Dallas open data: Building Permits. City of Dallas residential permits.
  • Los Angeles, CA: City of Los Angeles open data: Building Permits Issued from 2020 to Present. City of Los Angeles building permits for one- and two-family dwellings. Other cities in LA County are not included. Supplemental and revision permits are left out.
  • Chicago, IL: City of Chicago open data: Building Permits. City of Chicago permits whose descriptions identify residential work. Chicago records have no ZIP code, so areas are the city's 77 community areas.
  • Sacramento, CA: City of Sacramento open data: Building Permits Issued (current year). City of Sacramento residential permits. Unincorporated Sacramento County is not included. The city publishes this feed with a delay of a few weeks.

Method

Each week we download the last few months of residential permits from every feed and sort each permit into a job type, using the permit type and the scope of work the applicant filed: new homes, pools and spas, additions and remodels, ADUs, HVAC, solar, re-roofs and so on. Permits that don't fit a tracked job type are left out of the counts.

Some feeds arrive late. For each metro we use the latest date whose trailing week holds at least 60% of a normal week's records, and count the four weeks to that date. The previous four weeks are shown for comparison.

A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Supplemental and revision permits are left out where the feed marks them.

What we don't publish

These pages show counts by job type and ZIP code (or community area in Chicago), and the companies named on the most permits. A company is listed only when its name reads as a business or appears on three or more permits, so homeowners who pull their own permits never appear. The pages never show owner names, phone numbers or street addresses, even where the public record includes them.
