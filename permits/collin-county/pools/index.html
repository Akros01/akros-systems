About these numbers

Source: Collin Central Appraisal District: Building Permits (data.texas.gov). Residential permits that Frisco, Allen, McKinney, Wylie, Prosper, Celina and the other Collin County cities report to the appraisal district. Cities report permits to the appraisal district in monthly batches, so the latest weeks fill in late and the counts here run low until they do. For that reason we don't compare them with the weeks before.

Counts run through Nov 9, 2026, the latest date with a full week of records in the feed. We sort each permit into a job type from its permit type and filed scope of work. A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Cities revise records after they publish them, and a permit is not a guarantee of a job.

These pages show counts and the companies named on permits. They do not list owner names, phone numbers or street addresses. How we build these pages.
