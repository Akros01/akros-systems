How contractors use permit data

A residential permit is filed while a job is still being planned. For the trades that come after it, a new pool, a new house or an addition is a sign that an owner will soon be choosing pool service, security, landscaping, fencing, window coverings or HVAC work. These pages show where that activity is happening. The addresses themselves are in the paid products below.
