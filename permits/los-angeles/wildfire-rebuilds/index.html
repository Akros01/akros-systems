About these numbers

Source: City of Los Angeles open data: Building Permits Issued from 2020 to Present. City of Los Angeles building permits for one- and two-family dwellings. Other cities in LA County are not included. Supplemental and revision permits are left out.

Counts run through Nov 10, 2026, the latest date with a full week of records in the feed. We sort each permit into a job type from its permit type and filed scope of work. A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Cities revise records after they publish them, and a permit is not a guarantee of a job.

These pages show counts and the companies named on permits. They do not list owner names, phone numbers or street addresses. How we build these pages.
