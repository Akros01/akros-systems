About these numbers

Source: City of Sacramento open data: Building Permits Issued (current year). City of Sacramento residential permits. Unincorporated Sacramento County is not included. The city publishes this feed with a delay of a few weeks.

Counts run through Oct 25, 2026, the latest date with a full week of records in the feed. We sort each permit into a job type from its permit type and filed scope of work. A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Cities revise records after they publish them, and a permit is not a guarantee of a job.

These pages show counts only. They do not list owner names, phone numbers or street addresses. How we build these pages.
