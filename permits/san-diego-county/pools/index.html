About these numbers

Source: San Diego County open data: Building Permits. Residential permits issued by San Diego County for its unincorporated communities.

Counts run through Nov 12, 2026, the latest date with a full week of records in the feed. We sort each permit into a job type from its permit type and filed scope of work. A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Cities revise records after they publish them, and a permit is not a guarantee of a job.

These pages show counts and the companies named on permits. They do not list owner names, phone numbers or street addresses. How we build these pages.
