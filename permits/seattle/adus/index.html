About these numbers

Source: City of Seattle open data: Building Permits. City of Seattle building permits for single-family homes and duplexes.

Counts run through Nov 13, 2026, the latest date with a full week of records in the feed. We sort each permit into a job type from its permit type and filed scope of work. A permit is counted once, on its issue date. Cities revise records after they publish them, and a permit is not a guarantee of a job.

These pages show counts and the companies named on permits. They do not list owner names, phone numbers or street addresses. How we build these pages.
