About these reports

Source: NOAA Storm Prediction Center storm reports. Spotters, emergency managers and the public report hail of an inch or more, damaging wind and tornadoes; a report marks where a storm was seen, not every street it hit. Reports are preliminary and can be revised. A storm day runs from 12:00 UTC to 12:00 UTC the next day. Each report is tagged with the ZIP code whose center is nearest, from the Census Bureau's ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.

Akros Systems · Massachusetts, USA. Counts come from public open-data permit feeds and are rebuilt every week. These pages publish no owner names, phone numbers or street addresses.
